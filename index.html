the last page ✦ a letter for my princess

One little envelope.
A thousand things I mean.

There's something I've been saving just for you.

to my princess♡

My dearest Pari,

I hope you never forget that you are the heroine of your own story. Keep chasing the dreams that make your eyes light up, keep asking questions, keep being determined, curious, opinionated and wonderfully you. I don't want to be the reason your world gets smaller; I want to be the person cheering as it grows.

Wherever this world takes us — through new cities, busy seasons, unexpected turns or miles between us — I want to keep finding my way back to you. Not because every day will be effortless, but because I want us to keep choosing each other, speaking honestly, learning gently and building something that feels like home.

I can't wait for the day we build our own little castle. Maybe it won't have towers or a ballroom, but I hope it has a lot of laughter, books in every corner, silly arguments over which song to play, and enough room for all the dreams we're still figuring out. I want to dance with you to our favourite songs — in our living room, in the kitchen, or anywhere the music finds us — and make ordinary days feel magical simply because we're sharing them.

And wherever our adventures lead, my favourite place will always be beside you. I want to return to you, my princess, and keep making a life that is ours.

Until our next chapter, and all the ones after it,

Your prince, Devansh ♡
